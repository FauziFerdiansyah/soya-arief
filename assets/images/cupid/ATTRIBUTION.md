# Cupid animation artwork

The illustrations used by the footer cupid animation come from
[Microsoft Fluent Emoji](https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji),
licensed under the MIT License.

Files:

- `baby-angel.svg` — Fluent Emoji "Baby angel" (Color)
- `red-heart.svg` — Fluent Emoji "Red heart" (Color)
- `heart-with-arrow.svg` — Fluent Emoji "Heart with arrow" (Color)

These files are loaded on demand, only when a visitor taps the footer heart,
so they do not affect the invitation's initial page load.
